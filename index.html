PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making reservation tasks simpler

The Railway Reservation System is a menu-driven application developed in C++. It provides passenger services such as viewing available trains, booking tickets, cancelling tickets and checking ticket status through a unique PNR. Administrator options allow new trains to be added and all bookings to be viewed.

Important booking rules

PNR: starts at 1001 and increments for new tickets.

Senior citizen concession: passengers aged 60 or above pay 60% of the calculated fare.

Cancellation: 80% of the ticket amount is refunded; 20% is the cancellation charge.

Seat validation: booking is accepted only when requested seats are available.
